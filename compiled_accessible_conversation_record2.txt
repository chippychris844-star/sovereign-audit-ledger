# 2. Waste Tax framework

## Definition developed

Waste Tax is the cost of AI activity that does not convert into verified usable completion.

### Key formula

Actual Consumption % - Baseline Completion Cost % = Waste Tax %

Example:

236% actual consumption - 100% baseline = 136% Waste Tax

## Related categories

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waste Tax | Broad cost of AI activity that does not produce verified output |
| Non-Completion Tax | Paid AI process that fails to reach completion |
| Human Waste Tax | User time, stress, rework, evidence gathering, and support burden |
| Access-Completion Tax | Paying for access/seats/credits while actual completion remains blocked |
| Over-Completion Consumption | More than 100% of expected cost consumed with no verified completion |
| Credit Shrinkage | Loss of usable credit value through failed runs, repair loops, or blocked sessions |
| Rapid Post-Top-Up Credit Depletion | Paid top-up consumed quickly without clear verified output |

## Productivity rule

AI should not be called productive merely because it generated output. It is only productive when the output is:

1. verified;
2. usable;
3. reduces human burden;
4. reaches completion.

# 12. Formal Completion Ledger demand

The proposed ledger demand:

For every paid AI action:

1. what the user requested;
2. what the system charged;
3. what tool/agent/model acted;
4. whether the action completed;
5. what output was delivered;
6. whether the output was accessible/exportable;
7. whether prior work was broken or regressed;
8. whether human rework was required;
9. whether credits were restored or refund was considered;
10. whether the action contributed to recognized revenue/ARR.

Core line:

Without this ledger, AI companies can report monetized activity while hiding non-completion.
