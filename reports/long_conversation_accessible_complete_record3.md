# B. Core terms developed in the conversation

## Waste Tax

Waste Tax is the portion of AI activity, spend, compute, credit burn, subscription access, user labour, or rework that does not produce verified usable completion.

Formula:

```text
Waste Tax % = Actual Consumption % - Baseline Completion Cost %
```

Example:

```text
236% actual consumption - 100% baseline = 136% Waste Tax
```

## Non-Completion Tax

Paid AI/system activity that fails to reach a verified outcome.

## Human Waste Tax

The unpaid human labour transferred back to the user:
- re-prompting;
- debugging;
- checking;
- screenshotting;
- exporting;
- evidence gathering;
- chasing support;
- requesting refunds;
- rebuilding broken work;
- emotional/cognitive burnout.

## Access-Completion Tax

The cost created when a user pays for nominal access — seats, subscriptions, plans, credits — but actual usable completion remains separately metered, blocked, rate-limited, or failed.

## Over-Completion Consumption

A state where more than the expected full completion cost is consumed while completion is still not reached.

Short line:

**More than 100% cost consumed, 0% verified completion delivered.**

## Rapid Post-Top-Up Credit Depletion

A category created after the user showed Accio top-up evidence where a $50.53 payment was followed by roughly 80% of a 25,050 credit package showing as consumed within minutes.

## Completion Ledger

The missing record that should show, for every AI action:

1. what was requested;
2. what was charged;
3. what model/tool/agent acted;
4. whether the action completed;
5. what output was delivered;
6. whether output was accessible/exportable;
7. whether prior work was broken/regressed;
8. whether human rework was required;
9. whether credits/refunds were restored;
10. whether the action contributed to recognized revenue/ARR.
